# Carbon-Emission-Dashboard

This [Carbon Emission Dashboard](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMTMzMTcyMTktNjQyMy00OWEzLWFkZTYtMWFlNWQ3Y2YyODY4IiwidCI6ImE1ZjVhMjkzLWFhZjItNGMzYy04NWYxLTUyYjkxYTdlY2EyZSIsImMiOjEwfQ%3D%3D) tracks total emissions, yearly trends, emission reduction, and major emission sources from 2015–2022. It highlights manufacturing as the largest contributor and shows how emissions changed over time for better decisions.

## Problems Answered by the Dashboard
**1. How have total carbon emissions changed over time?**

The dashboard shows a clear downward trend from 2015 to 2022, with emissions decreasing to approximately 20.3M in 2022.

**2. How much have emissions decreased since the starting year?**

Total emissions decreased by approximately 18.1M, representing a 47.2% reduction compared with the starting year.

**3. Which category contributes the most carbon emissions?**

Manufacturing is the largest emission source at approximately 13.4M, accounting for around 66% of total emissions in 2022.

**4. What are the other major sources of emissions?**

After manufacturing, the biggest contributors are Product Use at 4.9M and Product Transportation at 1.9M, while other sources contribute about 0.1M.

**5. What is the average yearly reduction in carbon emissions?**

The dashboard indicates an average emission reduction of approximately 2.6M per year since 2015, showing consistent progress in reducing overall emissions.

## Conclusion

The dashboard shows that carbon emissions decreased significantly from 2015 to 2022, reaching about 20.3M in 2022. This represents a 47.2% reduction from the starting year. However, manufacturing remains the biggest source of emissions, contributing around 66% of the total, which makes it the main area to focus on for further reduction.

## Recommendations
**Focus on Manufacturing Emissions**
<br>Prioritize cleaner production methods, energy-efficient equipment, and renewable energy because manufacturing contributes approximately 13.4M emissions.
<br><br>**Reduce Product Use Emissions**
<br>Improve product energy efficiency and develop lower-emission products to reduce the 4.9M emissions generated during product use.
<br><br>**Improve Transportation Efficiency**
<br>Optimize delivery routes, use lower-emission transportation, and improve logistics planning to reduce the 1.9M transportation emissions.
<br><br>**Set Annual Reduction Targets**
<br>Continue monitoring yearly performance and establish measurable emission-reduction targets to maintain the downward trend.
<br><br>**Investigate Periods of Slow Improvement**
<br>Emissions remained around 22.5M from 2020 to 2021, so the company should identify what caused the slowdown and adjust its sustainability strategy accordingly.
